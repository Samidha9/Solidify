## 3 Biggest Differences

**1. Counter placement: right wall vs. floating island**
- *Photo:* The counter is pushed flush against the right wall, running from foreground to back, with the jukebox, condiments, and napkin dispensers on top and stools only on the customer (aisle) side. Menu boards hang on the wall directly above it.
- *Reconstruction:* The counter sits as a freestanding island in the middle of the room, with stools on the far side and a stray round pedestal table with stools in the near foreground. There's a dead zone between the counter and the right wall.
- *Fix:* Translate the counter right until it abuts the wall beneath the menu boards; delete the round table/stool cluster; keep stools on the aisle side only.

**2. Camera angle and height**
- *Photo:* Shot from seated eye level (~1.2 m), positioned at the front-left near the window, aimed diagonally down the room — the nearest booth back fills the lower-left foreground, and the exit door sits right of center. Strong asymmetric two-point perspective.
- *Reconstruction:* Camera is much higher (~1.7 m+) and centered in the aisle, aimed straight down the length of the room for a symmetric, flat composition.
- *Fix:* Drop the camera to ~1.2 m, move it to the front-left corner by the first booth, and yaw right ~25–30° so the view runs diagonally toward the back door.

**3. Booth configuration on the left wall**
- *Photo:* True diner booths — a row of U-shaped bays, each with two facing padded benches and one rectangular chrome-edged table between them, tucked under the window wall.
- *Reconstruction:* Continuous straight banquettes against the wall with small detached tables (and what look like loose chairs opposite them).
- *Fix:* Replace the banquettes with paired facing benches sharing one table per bay, aligned under the left windows; make tables larger with chrome edge banding.

**Minor fixes:** The back wall should be solid pink with the teal exit door (not dark glazing); add the blue "DINER" neon and pink clock on the left wall above the booths; the pendant should be a small globe hung over the booths near the window, not a large cone mid-ceiling.
