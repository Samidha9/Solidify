# Three Biggest Differences

**1. The layout is mirrored (camera is looking from the wrong end / room is flipped)**
In the photo, the row of booths runs along the **left** wall under the windows, and the counter with stools is on the **right**. In the reconstruction, this is reversed — counter and stools on the left, seating on the right, window on the right wall. Fix: rotate the camera 180° or mirror the floor plan so the booths sit under the windows on the left and the counter runs along the right.

**2. Booth geometry and orientation are wrong**
The photo shows tall, double-sided teal vinyl booths running *parallel* down the length of the room, with tables attached between facing bench seats. The reconstruction has low, backless bench seating pushed flat against the walls with loose freestanding tables, plus two small booth units against the far wall facing the camera. Fix: replace the wall benches with proper booth units — tall padded backrests (~1.2m), two facing benches per table, arranged in a row running away from the camera along the window wall.

**3. Scale and proportions are off**
The checkered floor tiles are roughly 2–3× too large compared to the photo, and the room reads as a squat, wide box rather than a long, relatively narrow diner with a taller ceiling. The stools and tables also look undersized relative to the floor area. Fix: reduce tile size to roughly 30–40cm squares, elongate the room along the booth axis, raise the ceiling height, and scale stools/tables up so seat heights land around 65–75cm.
