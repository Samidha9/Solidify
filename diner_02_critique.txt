**1. Camera position and angle**
- Real photo: camera is off-axis, standing at the front-left near the window, angled across the room (two-point perspective). The left wall with booths recedes to the right, and the counter runs along the right edge, with the "DINER" sign wall centered in the background.
- Reconstruction: camera is dead-center in the aisle, looking straight down the room (one-point perspective).
- **Fix:** Move the camera to the left side of the room near the front window and rotate it diagonally toward the back-right corner so the counter hugs the right edge of frame and the back wall (with the exit door) is visible off to the right.

**2. Booths are wrong — no tables, wrong structure**
- Real photo: individual booth units along the left wall, each with facing bench seats and a marble-topped table with chrome trim between them. There's also a standalone table in the foreground.

- Real photo: small black-and-white checkerboard tiles (dozens visible across the aisle width).
- Reconstruction: oversized gray-and-white checker tiles (only ~6–8 across the aisle), which makes the room feel much larger and emptier than it is.
- **Fix:** Reduce tile size to roughly a quarter of the current size and change the dark tiles to black, not gray.
